Clear information. A more prepared team.

  • 01Operations

    Daily Operations

    The everyday modules of the current application, shared across the team. What each person sees follows their role.

    • InventoryItems, locations and movements, with stock shown from verified counts.
    • PurchasingDraft, approve and receive orders. Nothing is sent to suppliers automatically.
    • RecipesA recipe library that shows what can be made from verified stock.
    • Stock CountPhone-first counting, one item at a time. Stock changes after a manager verifies.
    • ShiftsPlan and publish schedules; staff confirm shifts and request time off.
    • ReportsStock, purchasing, recipes, waste and labour. Sales data is not connected.

    Implemented

    Purchasing and Reports are for managers and administrators.

  • 02Assistance

    Alcedo AI

    Ask by text or voice, or add a photo or document. It answers from your venue’s records and prepares drafts — orders, counts, recipes — for a person to approve.

    • Text
    • Voice
    • Photos & files

    Implemented

    Mascot demonstration · not connected to the assistant

    Ready to help

  • 03Ingredients

    Food Intelligence

    A Flavor Map of ingredient pairings, each labelled with where it comes from, plus substitutes and ideas based on verified stock. Recipe drafts stay inactive until a manager approves them.

    Preview

  • 04Learning

    Training & Knowledge

    Alcedo Training: managers publish short video lessons with steps, aimed at the right roles; staff watch, resume and mark lessons complete. Knowledge: a searchable library with required reading.

03 Inside the workspace

See the modules as they are today.

Screenshots from the current application running against built-in test data: every name, quantity and price is sample data, not a real venue’s records.

Inventory

Know what is on hand before service.

  • Items by location, category and supplier
  • Stock taken from the last verified count plus recorded movements
  • Par levels flag what is low or out

Implemented

Purchasing

Orders that a person approves.

  • Suggested orders from items below par
  • Approve, mark as ordered and receive deliveries
  • Nothing is sent to a supplier automatically

Implemented · managers and administrators

Recipes

See what you can make tonight.

  • Recipe library by category
  • Availability worked out from verified stock
  • Costs visible to managers only

Implemented

Stock count

Count on a phone, one item at a time.

  • Guided counts by area
  • Quick part-bottle entries
  • Stock changes only after a manager verifies the count

Implemented

Shifts

A schedule everyone can see.

  • Week and month planning, published when ready
  • Staff confirm shifts, share availability and request time off
  • Changes after publishing are highlighted

Implemented

Reports

A clear view of stock and spend.

  • Overview, stock, purchasing, recipes, waste and labour
  • Items that need attention
  • Sales are not connected (no point-of-sale link)

Implemented · managers and administrators

Alcedo AI

Ask, check, then approve.

  • Text, voice, photos and documents
  • Answers show the records they are based on
  • Prepares drafts; a person approves every change

Implemented

Food Intelligence

Pairings grounded in what you stock.

  • Flavor Map of ingredient pairings with their sources
  • Substitutes and ideas from verified stock
  • Recipe drafts saved inactive until a manager approves

Preview

Alcedo Training

Short lessons for every role.

  • Video lessons with steps, aimed at roles
  • Staff resume and mark lessons complete
  • Managers see who has completed each version
